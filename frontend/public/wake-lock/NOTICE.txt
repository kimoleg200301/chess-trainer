Silent wake-lock media from NoSleep.js 0.12.0
https://github.com/richtr/NoSleep.js/tree/v0.12.0
https://www.npmjs.com/package/nosleep.js/v/0.12.0

keep-awake.webm and keep-awake.mp4 are extracted unchanged from src/media.js.
The detached inline-video playback/seek workaround is adapted in useScreenWakeLock.ts.
The legacy iOS page-reload workaround is not used.

Both files contain a silent audio track. The track is kept unmuted for browser
sleep prevention; no audible sound is present. Browsers may still use a media
session for playback. Native Screen Wake Lock is preferred whenever available.
