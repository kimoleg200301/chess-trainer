The bundled MP3 is an unmodified public HQ preview of a real chess piece
being placed on a wooden board. No account or runtime network access is needed.

Derived files: src/sounds/move.wav and src/sounds/capture.wav.
Rebuild: python frontend/scripts/generate-sounds.py (requires ffmpeg).
Processing: excerpt 2.535-2.815 seconds, 80 Hz high-pass, 1 ms fade-in,
40 ms fade-out, mono 22050 Hz PCM16, peak normalized to 0.72.
Capture pairs a quieter contact with the same placement 90 ms later.
No pitch shift or time stretching. Wrong/complete cues remain original
generated effects, unrelated to this third-party recording.
